Exploring Moduli of Tori: Isotopies and Area Distortion

Moduli of Tori

Here we view different geometric structures on a torus. One aspect of this change is the metric distortion — how much the surface area of small neighborhoods stretches or shrinks during the transformation. This is indicated by the color of the faces in the 3D model, where blue indicates shrinking and red indicates stretching. The histogram on the right quantifies this distortion across all faces, showing the distribution of area change.
